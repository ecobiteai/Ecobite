Three steps, moving in real time

EcoBite runs continuously in the background of a restaurant, grocer, or kitchen's normal operations — no extra work required to take part.

01 — Predict

Forecasting surplus before it's thrown out

Models trained on sales patterns, expiry windows, and delivery schedules flag surplus at restaurants, grocers, and farms hours or days before it would otherwise go to waste.

02 — Connect

Matching surplus to real need, instantly

The moment surplus is flagged, EcoBite matches it with nearby shelters, food banks, and community fridges based on what they need, how much they can store, and how close they are.

03 — Redistribute

Getting it there while it still matters

Optimized pickup and delivery routes get volunteers and drivers moving fast, with every handoff tracked from donor to recipient so nothing gets lost in between.
